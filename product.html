Product

Ed Technolog builds lightweight tools that help schools and teachers plan lessons, track student progress, and run online learning without wrestling with complicated systems. Our products are designed with classroom teachers and used by small and mid-sized schools across North America.

PlanBoard

A drag-and-drop lesson planner that lets teachers build units, share them with colleagues, and align them to standards.

  • Weekly and unit views on one screen
  • Built-in standards library for US state frameworks
  • Share and copy plans across a department
  • Export to PDF for substitute teachers

Markbook

A progress tracker that turns grades, quiz results, and teacher notes into a clear picture of each student.

  • Mastery-based and percentage grading
  • Flags students who are slipping before report cards
  • Parent-friendly progress summaries
  • Imports rosters from CSV files

Classroom Loop

An online learning space for assignments, discussions, and live sessions, designed to work on older school laptops and slow connections.

  • Low-bandwidth mode for rural and shared connections
  • Assignment inbox with simple feedback tools
  • Built-in video room for up to 60 students
  • Works on Chromebooks, tablets, and phones
