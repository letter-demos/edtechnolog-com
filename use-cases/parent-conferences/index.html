Steps to prepare student progress summaries in ProgressTrack.

Prepare for parent conferences

Step 1: Open the class in ProgressTrack and select the reporting period.

Step 2: Review mastery by standard and flag students who need discussion.

Step 3: Add a short teacher note for each student.

Step 4: Export one-page summaries as PDF.

Step 5: Print or send them before each meeting.
