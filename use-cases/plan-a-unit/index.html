Steps to build a standards-aligned unit plan in PlanBoard.

Plan a unit in an afternoon

Step 1: Create a new unit and choose a template, such as a three-week inquiry unit.

Step 2: Add standards from the built-in list for your grade and subject.

Step 3: Break the unit into daily lessons and drag in activities and materials.

Step 4: Share the plan with co-teachers and collect comments.

Step 5: Duplicate the unit next term and adjust.
