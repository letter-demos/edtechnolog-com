Why we built Classroom Loop for slow connections

When we visited rural schools in 2021, we saw students joining class from parking lots outside libraries because home internet could not hold a video call.

That changed our priorities. Classroom Loop has a low-bandwidth mode that drops video quality, caches assignments, and lets students submit work when the connection returns.

We also test on three-year-old Chromebooks, because that is what many schools actually own.

The result is less flashy than some competitors, but students stay connected, and that is the point.
