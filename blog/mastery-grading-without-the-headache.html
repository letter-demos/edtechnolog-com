Mastery grading without the headache

Mastery grading asks a simple question: can the student do the thing yet? It replaces averages that hide growth with a clearer picture of what a student knows.

Many schools hesitate because the change sounds large. You can start small, with one course and four or five skills.

Keep the scale short. Three or four levels are easier for students and parents to understand than ten.

Markbook lets you run mastery and percentage grades side by side, so you can pilot the approach before committing.
