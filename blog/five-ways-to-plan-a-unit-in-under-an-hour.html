Five ways to plan a unit in under an hour

Planning a unit can feel like it needs a free weekend. It does not have to. Most of the time goes to decisions you can make once and reuse.

Start with the end. Write the one thing students should be able to do when the unit finishes, then work backward to three or four checkpoints.

Next, borrow before you build. Check what colleagues have already made, and copy their plans into your own space to adjust.

Finally, schedule the assessments first and fill in the lessons between them. Teachers who do this in PlanBoard report finishing a unit outline in around fifty minutes.
