HubSpot Integration Setup | Ed Technolog

Sync contacts and company records with HubSpot.

Setup

Step 1: In Settings, open Integrations and choose HubSpot.

Step 2: Sign in and approve access for your HubSpot account.

Step 3: Select the contact list to sync.

Step 4: Map properties and start the sync.

Sync

Contacts and companies sync from HubSpot into Ed Technolog hourly. Plan and trial status sync back as company properties.

Limits

Up to 25,000 contacts per list. Custom objects and deal records are not synced. Property mapping supports text, number and date fields only.
