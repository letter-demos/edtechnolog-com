Salesforce Integration Setup | Ed Technolog

Sync school contacts and enrollment data with Salesforce.

Setup

Step 1: In Settings, open Integrations and choose Salesforce.

Step 2: Sign in with a Salesforce admin account.

Step 3: Map Ed Technolog fields to Contact and Account fields.

Step 4: Run the first sync and review the summary.

Sync

Contacts and accounts sync from Salesforce into Ed Technolog every 30 minutes. Trial status and plan name sync back to a custom field on the Account.

Limits

Up to 50,000 contacts per sync. Custom objects are not supported. Deleted records are not removed automatically. Sandbox orgs can be connected for testing.
