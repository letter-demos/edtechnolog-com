Slack Integration Setup | Ed Technolog

Connect Slack to receive Ed Technolog notifications.

Setup

Step 1: In Settings, open Integrations and choose Slack.

Step 2: Sign in as a Slack workspace admin and approve access.

Step 3: Pick a channel for notifications.

Step 4: Choose which events to send, such as new submissions or shared plan comments.

Data flow

Data flows one way, from Ed Technolog to Slack. Messages include event type, class name, and assignment or plan title. Student names are excluded unless you turn them on.

Limits

One channel per school. Messages cannot be answered from Slack. Delivery can take up to one minute. Private channels require inviting the Ed Technolog app first.
