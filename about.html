About Ed Technolog

Ed Technolog started in 2019 when two former teachers, Helen Okafor and Joel Brandt, got tired of watching colleagues spend Sunday evenings fighting software that was built for administrators, not classrooms. They began with a single lesson planner built in Helen's spare bedroom in Madison.

Early users kept asking for ways to track progress and run lessons online, so the product grew into three tools that share one account and one roster. Every feature still starts with a teacher describing a problem in their own words.

Today the team of 14 works remotely from the Midwest, supports schools in 31 states and two Canadian provinces, and still answers support emails within one school day.

What we value

  • Teachers first, always
  • Simple beats powerful
  • Work on the hardware schools actually have
  • Student data belongs to students and schools, never to advertisers
