Three Ed Technolog options for lesson planning, progress tracking and online learning.

Choose the option that fits your school

Ed Technolog offers three lightweight options built with classroom teachers: PlanBoard for lesson planning, ProgressTrack for student progress tracking, and ClassLink Online for running online learning.

PlanBoard and ProgressTrack are available on the pricing page and in the documentation. ClassLink Online is summarized on its overview page at /product/overview.

PlanBoard

Build weekly and unit lesson plans from reusable templates, align them to standards, and share them with co-teachers.

ProgressTrack

Record assessments, view mastery by student or class, and export progress summaries for parent conferences.

ClassLink Online

Host assignments, discussions and short live sessions for remote or blended classes. See /product/overview for capabilities and constraints.
