What ClassLink Online can and cannot do today.

ClassLink Online at a glance

ClassLink Online is our option for running online and blended classes without a heavy learning management system. It does not have a dedicated product page.

Capabilities

Teachers can post assignments with due dates, collect file and text submissions, run threaded class discussions, and schedule short live sessions with a join link. Students see one simple daily list of work. Grades flow into ProgressTrack when both are used.

Constraints

Live sessions support up to 60 participants. Uploaded files are limited to 100 MB each. There is no built-in proctoring, and course content cannot yet be imported from other learning management systems. Reporting is limited to completion and submission status.
